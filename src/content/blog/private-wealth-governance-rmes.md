Gary Bedford recently spoke at the Rocky Mountain Economic Summit in Victor, Idaho, presenting “Private Wealth Governance in a Wild Twenty-first Century” on July 16, 2026.

The speech considered how private wealth can be governed responsibly amid the volatility and institutional uncertainty of the twenty-first century. Drawing on the Wild Globalization project, Gary explored the obligations that accompany private wealth and the forms of stewardship, judgment, and governance needed to sustain it across generations.

Our thanks to the summit’s hosts at Bronze Buffalo Ranch for their warm hospitality and for bringing together a thoughtful community for the occasion.
